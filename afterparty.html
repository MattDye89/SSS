Everything you need for the party

Where to park, what to wear, and how the evening runs at Pearl's childhood home — 4710 E. 71st St, Indianapolis.

Getting there

Start here

There is no parking at 4710 E. 71st Street. We encourage you to use Uber or Lyft.

If you'd rather drive, all vehicles park along the West side of Dover Road, four houses west of 4710. Dover Road runs south from 71st Street — there is no parking on its east side. Available spaces will be marked. From there it's about a 150-yard walk through neighbor yards to the party — the route is marked with signs.

Someone will be there

Off-duty officers will be stationed at the driveway for rideshare arrivals and at Dover Road to escort those who drive.

What to wear

Festive Fall!

It's a wedding! So please, no jean shorts and crop tops — and yes, that means you too, Uncle Joe :) Beyond that, we want you comfortable, so wear whatever makes you feel the most awesome.

One practical note: there will be some walking through grass. Choose shoes accordingly.
